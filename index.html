Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Nolana ("we," "our," or "us") provides a personal ritual and habit-building app for iOS and Android. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Nolana, you agree to the practices described here.

Information we collect

CategoryExamplesWhy
Account information Name, email address, password (hashed, never stored in plain text) Create and secure your account, sign-in
Content you create Journal entries, habit and ritual selections, onboarding answers Personalize your ritual and track your progress
AI coach conversations Messages you send to the in-app AI coach Generate a personalized coaching response (via OpenAI)
Usage data App opens, screens viewed, feature interactions Understand usage and improve the app (via Firebase Analytics)
Device & push token Device identifier, push notification token Deliver reminders and notifications (via Firebase Cloud Messaging)

How we use your information

  • To provide, personalize, and maintain your daily ritual experience
  • To sync your habits, journal, and favorites across sessions
  • To send reminders and notifications you've opted into
  • To understand aggregate usage and improve app features
  • To generate responses from the in-app AI coach
  • To respond to support requests

How we store and protect your data

Account and content data is stored on secure, encrypted infrastructure provided by our database host (Supabase, running on PostgreSQL). Passwords are stored using industry-standard hashing and are never stored in plain text. When you message the in-app AI coach, that message is sent to OpenAI solely to generate a response and is not used by us for any other purpose. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your information, though no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Third-party services

We use the following third-party services, which may process limited data on our behalf:

  • Firebase (Google) — app analytics and push notifications
  • Supabase — secure database hosting for your account and app content
  • OpenAI — processes your messages to the in-app AI coach to generate responses

These providers only receive the data necessary to perform their function and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

Your choices

  • You can edit or delete your journal entries and habit data at any time within the app
  • You can disable push notifications in your device settings
  • You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings → Delete Account within the app. This is irreversible.

Data retention

We retain your account and content data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal data within a reasonable period, except where retention is required by law.

Children's privacy

Nolana is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do. Continued use of Nolana after changes means you accept the updated policy.

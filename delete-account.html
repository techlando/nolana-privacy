Delete your account

How to delete your Nolana account and the data associated with it.
Privacy Policy Support

Option 1: Delete from within the app

  1. Open Nolana and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Delete Account.
  3. Confirm that you want to delete your account.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

No longer have the app installed? Email us from the address associated with your Nolana account with the subject "Delete my account." We'll confirm the request and process it.

landonator30@gmail.com

What is deleted

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time. This is irreversible. This includes:

  • Account information: name, email address, and password
  • Content you create: journal entries, habit and ritual selections, and onboarding answers
  • AI coach conversations: messages you sent to the in-app AI coach
  • Device & push token: device identifier and push notification token

Data retention

If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal data within a reasonable period, except where retention is required by law.

Want to remove only some of your data instead? You can edit or delete your journal entries and habit data at any time within the app.

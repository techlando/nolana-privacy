Common questions

How do I delete my account?

Open Nolana, go to Settings, and tap "Delete Account." This permanently removes your account and all associated data and cannot be undone.

I forgot my password — how do I reset it?

On the sign-in screen, tap "Forgot password?" and follow the instructions sent to your email.

How do I turn off notifications?

You can manage notification preferences from within the app, or disable notifications for Nolana entirely in your device's system settings.

I found a bug — how do I report it?

Email us at the address above with a description of what happened and, if possible, a screenshot. We read every message.

How do I request my data or ask about privacy?

See our Privacy Policy for details on what we collect and how it's used, or email us directly with any questions.
